# Format coverage compared with RECOIL

Generated by `scripts/coverage.py` from RECOIL's
[format list](https://recoil.sourceforge.net/formats.html) and
`retro-image --list-formats`. Do not edit by hand.

- RECOIL formats: 552

A row is covered when one of our formats on the same platform claims one
of its extensions; "check" means RECOIL lists more variants for that
extension than we register.
